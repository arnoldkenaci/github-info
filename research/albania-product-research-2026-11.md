# Product research: what to import and sell in Albania

*Research date: 22 November 2026. Prices: €1 = 89.78 lek (Bank of Albania, 20 Nov 2026).
Market facts and their sources are in `.claude/skills/albania-market-context/`.*

## Bottom line

Don't try to beat Temu on small, cheap items: consumers import those duty-free under €22, while your
stock pays 20% VAT and duty. Albania's real gaps are in **mid-priced, branded, bulky or trust-sensitive
products (about €150–1,100)**. These are items people want with a local warranty, cash on delivery and fast
delivery, and today they can only get them by ordering from Amazon (people often search "amazon albania",
"amazon de" and "amazon uk") or from Kosovo.

**Best first bets** (gap confirmed, demand driver, workable margin):

1. **Home backup power**: 1–2 kWh power stations (Bluetti, Jackery, EcoFlow Delta) plus folding panels.
2. **Premium baby gear**: Bugaboo, Joolz, Elvie, Nanit/Owlet. Cybex and Medela are sold locally at 1.5–2× EU prices.
3. **Specialty home coffee**: Gaggia, Sage and Lelit machines, good grinders and the accessories.
4. **Pet tech**: self-cleaning litter boxes and smart feeders (Petkit, Catlink).
5. **Ninja kitchen**: the Creami, dual-zone air fryers and Slushi, which are TikTok Shop's #1 brand in Germany, Spain and Italy.

You could start a single niche store around 1–2 of these, for example **"home and family premium"**
(baby + coffee + kitchen) or **"home resilience"** (power + dehumidifiers + water).

## How this was researched

- **Google Trends for Albania:** 48 products compared over 12 months, using an Albania option I added to the
  `google-trends` skill.
- **Availability checks:** about 30 products searched on Gjirafa50.al, Foleja, Merrjep, Shpresa, Globe,
  specialist shops and brand sites. Neptun and Intersport could not be searched directly.
- **Neighbouring markets:** best-seller and trend data from TikTok Shop EU, Trendyol, Skroutz, Amazon.it and Kosovo.
- **Market rules:** customs, VAT, payments, couriers and consumer law, from official sources (see the skill).

## Avoid (already crowded, or blocked)

- **Korean skincare**: many specialist shops and pharmacies already sell it.
- **Protein and creatine**: at least five dedicated shops; supplements also need food-authority (AKU) paperwork.
- **Starlink**: officially sold at Gjirafa50 and Neptun.
- **Robot vacuums**: Roborock has 24 models at EU prices; only premium Dreame and Ecovacs are missing.
- **70mai dash cams, Xiaomi and Dyson purifiers, standing desks**: already covered.
- **E-scooters**: banned from public roads since October 2025.
- **Balcony solar sold for savings**: power costs only about €0.09/kWh and the grid-connection rules are unclear.
  Sell panels only as part of backup bundles.
- **Anything under about €30 that Temu sells.**

## Rough unit economics (illustrative)

These are assumptions, not quotes. Replace them with real supplier prices before you buy anything.

- **Buying price:** the distributor sells at about 70% of the EU retail price before VAT.
- **Freight:** 4–15% of the buying price.
- **Customs duty:** 0% for EU-origin goods with proof of origin; 2.7–7.3% for Chinese-made goods.
- **Fees:** about €3 per unit for customs, broker and scanning.
- **VAT:** 20% on the selling price; you reclaim the import VAT as a VAT-registered importer.
- **Delivery:** €5–20 per order for courier and COD fees.
- **Refused orders:** 7% of COD orders are refused, costing a round-trip delivery.
- **Warranty:** 3% of sales set aside for the 2-year defect liability.
- **Ads:** €12–60 per sale.

## A low-risk way to test (4–6 weeks)

1. **Pick 2 niches** from the top 5 and request price lists from EU distributors or the brands' EU warehouses.
   Ask about exclusive rights for Albania, since several brands have no distributor there.
2. **Run demand tests before buying stock:** an Instagram/TikTok page and a simple landing page, €150–300 of ads
   per niche, and a pre-order or waitlist with cash on delivery. Count real pre-orders, not likes.
3. **Order a small batch** (5–20 units) of the winner. Use EU-origin goods where you can, and get the origin
   paperwork (EUR.1 or a statement on the invoice) so the goods come in duty-free.
4. **Offer your own 2-year warranty** and say so loudly. That is your edge over Temu and grey sellers.
5. **Before the first import**, see an accountant about QKB registration, VAT registration (importers may have to
   register at any turnover) and fiscal invoices on COD sales. Get COD payout terms from 2–3 couriers
   (Ultra C.E.P., Albanian Delivery Express, Posta Shqiptare).

## Open questions to check yourself

- Neptun.al and Intersport Albania stock, which we couldn't search; check by hand.
- Real wholesale prices and whether each brand already has an Albanian or Balkan distributor.
- Courier COD fees, how fast couriers pay out the cash, and refusal rates.
- The 2027 tax package (VAT 21%, lower VAT threshold): watch for the final vote.
- Breast pumps as medical devices (AKBPM) and the grid rules for plug-in solar (ERE/OSHEE).
