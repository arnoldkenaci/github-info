# More niches: does anything beat host starter kits?

## The answer in short

- **No. None of the six new niches beats host starter kits as a business of its own.** All three advisors said "no" for four of the six niches. Water filtration got one "maybe" (from the CFO) and EV charging gear got one "maybe" (from the marketer). Where the advisors disagree, this report takes the more careful view, which is "no" for every niche.
- **One niche can join host kits: Turkish kitchen packs** (a cookware set plus Pasabahce glasses). They go to the same hosts and property managers and travel on the same Turkish pallet with the same EUR.1 paperwork for 0% duty. Sell them only as a line on the host-kit order form, pre-sold with deposits. Do not sell them on Instagram to the public.
- **Do not open a second business yet.** Water filters need an installation service and EV adapters carry electrical-safety risk. Look at them again only after the first host-kit pallet has paid for itself and repeat B2B customers ask for them.
- **Reject outright:** dehumidifiers as an import, power stations and car-care chemicals. They lose money even on their own research numbers.
- **For winter (Oct-Mar):** offer a small "damp pack" to rental clients. Buy any dehumidifier locally, and only after the client has paid. Do not import it.
- **One correction to the benchmark itself:** the research used two ways to count unsold stock. Before you compare anything again, recalculate the host-kit stress test (+EUR 100) with unsold stock counted at cost.

## Best new niche in detail

**No new niche is good enough on its own.** The best idea is an **add-on to host kits: a Turkish "rental-flat kitchen pack"**, sold to the same hosts and property managers, on the same invoice and the same Turkish pallet.

### First order, with numbers

- **Research model (stand-alone, own pallet):** 20 basic + 10 premium packs, about 290 kg. Goods EUR 1,370, freight EUR 300, fixed costs EUR 242 (broker 100 + scanning 22 + EUR.1 and chamber of commerce 40 + labels 30 + translation 50). Cash needed EUR 1,987. If all sell: EUR 915 contribution (27%). Stress test +EUR 185 at cost, -EUR 422 written off, -EUR 83 pessimistic.
- **As an add-on (recommended):** put the packs on the **same Turkish pallet as the host kits**. The CFO estimates this saves about EUR 160 of the EUR 242 fixed cost and most of the EUR 300 freight, which improves the stress test by about EUR 300.
- **Size the order on deposits.** The coach says pre-sell at least 10 packs before ordering. Order only the pre-paid packs plus a small buffer. The 30-pack model is an upper limit, not a target. As a rough guide (simple arithmetic from the research landed costs), 7 basic + 3 premium packs land at about EUR 660. Add more if the stainless set costs more than EUR 60, subtract some when the pallet is shared, and add 20% import VAT that you get back later.

### Paperwork

- An EUR.1 or invoice origin declaration for every product line, and **"Made in Turkey" confirmed on every SKU**. Ask the Albanian broker which document the Albania-Turkey FTA needs for 7013 (glass) and 7323 (steel). Make this a condition of payment.
- A food-contact declaration of compliance from the supplier for coated pans and glass (Instruction No. 15/2012 under Food Law 9863). Ask the broker or AKU what is checked at import.
- Albanian labels on every unit: material, importer, care, induction and dishwasher suitability, food-contact symbol. Do not repeat "PFOA-free" or "granite" claims unless the supplier gives you evidence.
- The usual rules: VAT registration, 20% import VAT (recoverable), a real-time fiscal invoice for every sale, 14-day withdrawal right and 2-year seller liability (peeling non-stick coatings are a typical claim).

## Niches to avoid and why

- **Home backup power (score 13):** each unit costs EUR 420-590 landed, so the budget buys only 4-6 units. Duty is 10% on stations. Stations are lithium batteries, which count as Class 9 dangerous goods for freight, couriers and warranty returns. There is no service centre in Albania. Brands discount heavily in the EU, and Gjirafa50 and Foleja already stock EcoFlow and Anker. Most outages are short, and router UPS units, Temu power banks and generators are cheap substitutes. Stress test -EUR 273 at cost, -EUR 890 written off.
- **Compressor dehumidifiers as an import (score 14):** the market is saturated. Neptun, Megatek and Shpresa sell 16 L units for EUR 145-200 with 24-month instalments, at or below what you can land. They lose money on every sale to the public. Each unit weighs 13 kg, there is a 2-year compressor liability with no service network, and the season is short. Stress test -EUR 570 at cost. Use the local, prepaid version only (see above).
- **Premium car care (score 19):** official distributors already exist (GRT for Koch-Chemie and Ceramic Pro, Top Auto Parts for Sonax). Car washes buy 20 kg of shampoo for about EUR 50. Every product needs an Albanian chemical-hazard (CLP) label and safety data sheet (Law 27/2016, DCM 488/2016). The best-margin items are dangerous goods. Few people wash cars at home in flats. Stress test about -EUR 296 at cost.
- **EV charging gear (score 39), not now:** it uses unvetted Alibaba electrical gear on customers' cars, with fire liability and returns from cars it does not fit. Local sellers already exist, and car lots can import the same goods themselves at 0% duty. Pessimistic stress test -EUR 351. Look at it again only with tested samples, insurance and car-lot pre-orders.
- **Water filtration (score 40), not now:** it is an installation business. That means about 40 hours of plumbing visits, leak liability in flats and one-plumber dependence, against trusted local companies with free installation and trials. The realistic stress test is about EUR 0 to negative. Look at it again only as pre-sold installs for your B2B clients.
- **General rule (coach):** leave out anything that needs installing, carries a lithium battery, is a chemical that needs its own label, or plugs into a car. One mistake there can cost more than a year's profit.
