# Ninja (SharkNinja) suppliers for Albania

*Researched 22 November 2026. Contacts are the companies' public business addresses. Items marked
"unverified" need confirming by email or phone.*

## Key findings

1. **Comtrade Distribution is SharkNinja's appointed partner for South-East Europe, and its website lists
   Albania among its 14 countries.** It has no Albanian office or manager yet. It is the only route to
   official warranty support and to becoming the **authorised Ninja seller for Albania**.
2. **Duty is 10%, not ~3%.** Ninja is made in China (some lines in Vietnam), so the EU duty-free preference
   doesn't apply even when you buy from an EU company. Albania's general (MFN) rate is **10%** for air fryers
   (HS 8516.60), blenders and ice-cream makers (8509.40 / 8509.80), and **2%** if the Slushi is classed as
   refrigerating equipment (8418.69). Source: World Bank WITS, 2022 data; confirm with a customs broker.
3. **Grey stock has no SharkNinja warranty.** The guarantee is valid only in the country of purchase and only
   from authorised retailers. You still owe Albanian customers 2 years of defect liability.
4. **Use the current model codes.** AF300EU/AF400EU are being replaced by **DZ300EU/DZ400EU**, NC300EU by
   **NC302EU**, NC501EU by **NC502EU**, and the Creami Swirl is **NC701EU**. Insist on "EU" codes and CE marks;
   avoid "UK" plug versions.

## Does it make money? (see `ninja-landed-cost.py`)

Average unit, €200 EU street price, 10% duty, pallet freight, broker and fees, courier and COD, €20 ads per
sale, 7% refused COD orders, 4% warranty reserve:

| Supplier discount (vs street ex-VAT) | Units per shipment | Albanian price vs German street | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8% (small wholesaler) | 16 | +15% | **loss** (−12% to −51%) |
| 25–30% (official distributor) | 16 | +15% | loss (−5% to −10%) |
| 30% | 40 | +25% | ~12% |
| 30% | 40 | +35% | ~18% |
| 35% | 80 | +35% | ~25% |

**Conclusion:** Ninja is **not** profitable as a tiny grey-import test. It works only with:
- the official distributor's price (at least 30% off retail),
- 40+ units per shipment, or Comtrade delivering to Tirana together with other freight,
- Albanian prices about 25–35% above German street prices.

That kind of premium is normal for Albania when a product isn't available locally: power stations are sold
45–115% above EU prices and Cybex strollers about 60% above.

## Freight and import notes

- **Groupage from Germany to Tirana:** Mainfreight, Gebrüder Weiss (Tirana branch), DSV, LKW Walter,
  KLG Europe. **From Italy:** Bari–Durrës ferry, ~48 sailings a week. Albanian forwarders such as Dyrrahsped
  quote 5–7 days from Germany and 2 days from Greece.
- **Rough cost (unverified estimate):** €250–450 per Euro-pallet from Germany, €150–250 from Greece, plus
  €80–150 for a customs broker and the €22 scanning fee on declarations over €1,000.
  A pallet holds about 20 appliance boxes.
