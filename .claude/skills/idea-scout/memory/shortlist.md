## New this run

I-099 to I-108 (10 ideas): 8 shortlisted (50+), 2 new (I-101, I-105), none rejected. None scores above ~78; none
enters the top 10.
- I-103 sticky traps (74): GIZ counts ~3,000 greenhouse farms and biological control on ~250 ha, growing from
  "four farmers to hundreds" in a year, so the practice passes rule 21. But Megateksa sells retail traps, the ~4
  biocontrol providers probably sell them too, and Temu sells small packs. Only boxes and rolls for growers work.
- I-099 furniture fittings and edge banding (72): Megateksa sells runners (180-259 ALL) and edge banding (85 ALL/m),
  and Blum has a Tirana-area dealer (HODO-ALB). Hinges pay 15% MFN, so Turkish EUR.1 origin is a must.
- I-106 café terrace chairs (72): 17,742 food-service firms, but Palmiye Koçak has sold contract café furniture in
  Albania for 25 years, and chairs last 3-5 years.
- I-100 upholstery fabric (70): 0% duty, B2B by the roll, but only ~49 registered furniture makers, and Gjergji H
  Tekstil already lists upholstery cloth.
- I-107 life jackets (68): Border Police checked 1,241 boats in 2025 (+7%), but there is no uniform life-jacket rule yet,
  and Megateksa sells Yamaha jackets (2,699 ALL). An add-on to I-070 fenders, for the same boat operators.
- I-108 sheep shears (67): no dealer found, but the flock fell from 1,480k to 1,166k in four years (rule 10).
- I-102 hostel bunks and lockers (64): 163 hostels (Hostelz), one-off fit-outs; Megateksa sells bunk beds. It could
  go on the host-kit order form.
- I-104 water tanks (62): real need (Tirana water ~5 h/day), but Megateksa sells the full range (1,000 L 25,000 ALL),
  tanks ship mostly air, and local rotomoulders are likely.
- I-101 thermal receipt rolls (49, new): the POS-terminal mandate (31 Dec 2026) adds demand, but Printing House
  Progres makes cash-register rolls locally, so new rule 22 caps it at 49.
- I-105 minimarket gondola shelving (45, new): no minimarket count found (rule 5), and chains fit out direct.
