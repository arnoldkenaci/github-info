## New this run

I-089 to I-098 (10 ideas): all 10 shortlisted (50+), none new or rejected. None scores above ~78; none enters the top 10.
- I-091 leno mesh bags (74): no seller found, but potato output fell 7.2% and German imports cut prices (rule 10).
  Very cheap bags (rule 16), and without EUR.1 the duty is 10%.
- I-097 bale net wrap and silage film (73): no seller found, but there is no evidence that baling is common on small
  Albanian farms (new rule 21, demand 3), and the herd is shrinking.
- I-089 PV mounting structures (71): 180-210 MW of solar added in 2025, but a Durres maker (Pespa Alumin) already
  extrudes mounting systems, and net metering ends in January 2027.
- I-095 rebar spacers (69): Megateksa sells the Dakota range at 7-185 ALL. Only works inside a "site consumables"
  bundle with I-083 formwork plywood and I-061 scaffold netting.
- I-090 rental kayaks and pedal boats (68): no dealer found, but from 2025 beach plots go only to hotels and the number
  of operators is expected to fall ~60% (new rule 20).
- I-098 coco coir grow slabs (64): most greenhouses are soil-based (UNDP); Asian origin pays MFN duty.
- I-093 oak barrels (63), I-096 housekeeping trolleys (63), I-092 vineyard trellis consumables (62), I-094 dairy tubs
  (58): Megateksa already sells each of them (20 L oak barrel 13,990 ALL; cleaning trolleys 39,999-49,999 ALL;
  galvanised posts and cable; dairy buckets 100-420 ALL). These are add-on lines at most.
