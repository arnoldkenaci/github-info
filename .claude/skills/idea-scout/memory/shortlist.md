## New this run

I-176 to I-184 (9 ideas): 8 shortlisted (50+), 1 new (I-180), none rejected. None reaches ~78; none enters the top 10.
- I-181 logo café terrace umbrellas (71): 0% with Turkish/EU origin (10% MFN), bulky, dense café base, no maker or seller
  found (Megateksa garden umbrellas only, rule 30). But umbrellas are bought every few seasons, and coffee brands already
  give cafés branded equipment, so demand is held at 8 (rule 12). Pairs with I-106 terrace furniture and I-111 heaters.
- I-182 plastering-machine wear parts (70): 0%, no seller found, but no evidence that Albanian crews plaster by machine
  (rule 21) and machine dealers supply parts (rule 25). The score is a rubric artefact.
- I-183 Turkish welding electrodes and MIG wire (68): 0% with EUR.1, monthly repeat, but Megateksa already sells the same
  brand (Magmaweld, 2,290-4,990 ALL) and Würth has a welding range. New rule 34 cuts the gap to 3 points.
- I-184 parking-garage safety products (68): 0%, heavy, permits +24.8%, but Megateksa sells parking corners, speed humps
  and wheel stops, and each building buys once.
- I-179 low-tunnel hoops (67): no seller found, but Fier strawberries and Divjaka watermelons grow in simple greenhouses,
  not low tunnels (rule 21). Another rubric artefact.
- I-178 cheese cultures and rennet (66): 0%, monthly repeat, no distributor found (only Ubuy home packs). Food additive,
  so 0 regulation points (rule 27); big dairies import direct (rule 17). **Add-on to the I-064 small-dairy pallet.**
- I-177 ready-to-use bee feed (61): no seller found, 645k hives, but beekeepers can make sugar syrup themselves, a pallet
  is worth only ~EUR 925 (rule 16), and sugar may pay 10% duty. **Add-on to I-036 beekeeping.**
- I-176 PVC banner and printing vinyl (60): Algrafika already stocks banner rolls; mostly Chinese-made, so no duty edge.
- I-180 in-room hotel safes (45, new): 15% duty on Chinese safes, one-off, and the star classification that might require
  them has reportedly never been applied (rule 5).
