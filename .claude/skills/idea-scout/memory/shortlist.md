## Best candidates for the next deep-research batch

1. **I-040 glass jars (81), screened together with I-036 beekeeping equipment (79).** Still the strongest pair and not yet
   deep-dived. They share buyers: 645k hives (INSTAT 2025) and small olive-oil producers. First phone 3–5 agro and
   beekeeping shops and a honey co-op to test the "scarce" gap. *With host kits:* no shared buyers or supplier, but
   oil bottles sell in Nov–Jan, the host kits' quiet winter.
2. **I-050 plastic harvest crates (78), with I-048 greenhouse film and I-049 drip tape (75 each) as a "Turkish grower
   supplies" bundle.** They go to the same growers in Fier, Berat and Korca, and the same Turkish exporters ship them.
   Crates are bulky and lost every season, so Temu can't compete and buyers come back. First find the importers behind
   the ~900 t of crate imports and phone 3 packhouses. *With host kits:* no shared buyers. Film is changed in
   autumn–winter (unverified), so it could fill the host kits' quiet months, and Turkish freight could be shared.
3. **I-047 wine corks and capsules (77), screened together with I-038 winemaking equipment (74).** The corks are the
   repeat consumable that the equipment lacks (rule 11). Check whether small cellars buy from Italian makers direct, and
   what the new June 2026 excise-stamp-over-cap rule means for capsules. *With host kits:* no shared buyers; the
   Aug–Oct season follows the host season.

Runner-up: I-041 peshtemal for beach clubs (74), the most natural host-kit add-on (same Denizli mills, same EUR.1 pallet).
