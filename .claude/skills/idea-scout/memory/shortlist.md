## New this run

I-185 to I-194 (10 ideas): 8 shortlisted (50+), 2 new (I-186, I-191), none rejected. None reaches ~78; none enters the
top 10.
- I-192 roll-up banner stands, X-banners and pop-up walls for print shops (75): 0% duty from any origin (7616.99),
  repeat B2B, and Algrafika's visual-communications menu has no display hardware (rule 35 checked). But big print houses
  may import their own stands (rule 17) and AliExpress/Temu sell them, so the gap and margin need a phone check.
- I-185 logo entrance mats (74): Turkish nylon or rubber mats at 0% with EUR.1, custom and bulky, no local maker or
  seller found (Megateksa sells only retail doormats, rule 30). One-off B2B, replaced every 1-3 years. Same hotels and
  managers as host kits.
- I-193 greenhouse lock channel and wiggle wire (71): 0% duty, no seller found, but no evidence Albanian greenhouses fix
  film this way (rule 21), so a rubric artefact like I-179.
- I-190 coffins for funeral homes (69): 21,394 deaths in 2025, 0% duty, bulky, no maker found online. But many burials
  may use no coffin, the Tirana funeral agency sells coffins itself, and local carpenters very likely make them (rules
  12, 22, new rule 37).
- I-187 PV solar cable and MC4 connectors (66): solar boom (180-210 MW in 2025), 0% with Turkish origin, but the Tirana
  PV distributors (EuroElektra, Solaron, Nova Solar, Vega Solar) sell full kits to installers (rule 25).
- I-194 HACCP colour-coded cutting boards (66): Megateksa already sells professional PE boards in red, green and white.
- I-189 disposable car-interior protection kits (65): no seller found on Würth Albania's shop, ~100k cars imported in
  2025, but a pallet is worth under EUR 1,000 (rule 16) and packs sell cheaply online. Add-on to I-162 body-shop
  abrasives.
- I-188 logo event wristbands (64): no seller found, but there are few buyers and no attendance figures (rule 18).
- I-191 logo eyeglass cases (48, new): 15% duty on Chinese cases, Temu-cheap, frame wholesalers supply cases.
- I-186 key blanks for key-cutting kiosks (45, new): no Albanian dealer found, but no count of kiosks or locksmiths
  (rule 5).
- Dropped before screening: paper straws (no Albanian straw ban found, so no demand trigger).
