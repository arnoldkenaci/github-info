## New this run

I-168 to I-175 (8 ideas): 7 shortlisted (50+), 1 new (I-172), none rejected. None reaches ~78.
- I-170 mobile site fence panels (77): 0% duty, heavy steel (Temu-proof), builders reuse them and smaller builders could
  rent them. VKM 448/2025 makes site enclosure compulsory, but not mesh panels: demand held at 8 (new rule 32). Local
  welders could make panels (rule 22 risk). Shares buyers and a Turkish truck with I-061 scaffold netting and I-159
  façade mesh.
- I-169 tiller blades, plough shares and mower knives (74): 0%, wears out every season, sold through agro shops; gap
  available (Megateksa rotary tiller knife 250 ALL). First idea from EIMA-type farm-machinery fairs.
- I-168 espresso-machine wear parts (72): 0% Italian parts, dense café base, but coffee brands lend and service the
  machines, so buyers are technicians and roasters (rule 25), and small gaskets are easy to buy online.
- I-175 restaurant hood baffle filters (71): no seller found, but filters come with the hood from the installer and are
  mostly cleaned, not replaced (rule 25); local stainless fabricators are a rule-22 risk.
- I-171 water-filled road barriers (70): no seller found, cones are at Megateksa; no evidence Albanian road works use
  water-filled barriers (rule 21). Side line to I-170.
- I-173 rubber cow mats (67): 0%, bulky, no seller found, but cattle down 62,000 in five years (rule 10) and one-off
  buys. Add-on to the I-064 dairy pallet only.
- I-174 EN 840 wheeled waste bins (63): a draft 2026 separate-collection decision would need colour-coded bins, but
  municipalities buy by tender, bins are mostly air (rule 28) and local moulding is unchecked.
- I-172 ironer padding and laundry nets (45, new): no count of commercial laundries found (rule 5).
