## New this run

I-109 to I-117 (9 ideas): 7 shortlisted (50+), 2 new (I-110, I-116), none rejected. One (I-114) scores above ~78, but
on an unverified gap.
- I-114 logo-printed napkins and placemats (83): 0% duty, monthly repeat buys from a dense café base (17,742
  food-service firms; Albania 3rd in Europe for cafés). Three searches (Megateksa, web, registry) found no napkin printer
  or tissue converter. Rule 15: phone 2 Tirana print houses first; a local converter would make it saturated (rule 22).
  Goes on one order form with I-034 printed cups.
- I-115 olive-fly mass-trapping kits (72): the Ministry of Agriculture guide recommends one trap per two olive trees;
  no seller found. But kits with insecticide or pheromone lures are plant-protection products (new rule 24), and
  single traps are cheap.
- I-117 bitumen waterproofing membranes (71): 0% duty, steady builder demand, but Megateksa (Bituline 4,550 ALL) and
  Sika Albania already sell them.
- I-109 stainless olive-oil and wine tanks (66): Megateksa already sells 200-300 L tanks (21,999-27,999 ALL); one-off
  buys. 7309 pays 12% MFN, so Italian origin proof is a must.
- I-113 Turkish bridal gowns for bridal shops (64): 0% duty and Izmir is the bridal hub, but a Mon Cheri stockist is in
  Tirana and there is no count of shops or weddings.
- I-112 artificial turf (60): ACP and Megateksa sell it; FSHF and municipalities build pitches centrally (rule 12).
- I-111 café patio heaters (57): Megateksa sells one; 10% duty and most heaters are Chinese-made. A winter add-on for
  café buyers.
- I-110 restaurant charcoal (49, new): about USD 0.5m of imports a year, but local kilns make charcoal (rule 22) and a
  pallet is worth under EUR 1,000 (rule 16).
- I-116 nursery pots (45, new): no nursery count (rule 5).
