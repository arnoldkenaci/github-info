## New this run

I-118 to I-127 (10 ideas): 8 shortlisted (50+), 2 new (I-118, I-124), none rejected. None reaches ~78.
- I-121 logo-printed courier mailer bags (73): online shops reorder monthly; e-commerce ~US$307m (+10-15% a year).
  No local seller found in 3 searches (unverified). Two open risks: whether VKM 367/2022 (plastic-bag ban) covers
  mailers, and whether couriers hand out free bags. Paper mailers avoid the first risk.
- I-119 RFID hotel key cards (70): 0% duty, no Albanian card supplier found (unverified), but cards must match each
  hotel's lock system and many hotels buy from their lock installer.
- I-127 printed beer mats (69): 0% duty, no local printer found, but the pallet value is tiny (rule 16) and drink
  brands may give mats free (unverified). A line on the café order form only.
- I-126 hardware for aluminium/PVC window fabricators (65): repeat per job, 15% MFN so Turkish EUR.1 is a must; K-AKS
  (Durres) already wholesales profiles and accessories, and system houses (Schüco, Aluplast, Alumil) bundle hardware
  with their profiles (new rule 25 cut it from 72).
- I-120 snow chains (66): Megateksa sells textile chains (2,539-2,739 ALL); police bar cars without chains on snowy
  roads. 15% MFN unless EU-made. A winter line through tyre shops and rental fleets.
- I-122 hotel guest amenities (64): same buyers as host kits, but cosmetics paperwork (Law 26/2017) and a
  self-described big local supplier.
- I-123 sausage casings (58): veterinary certificate and AKU rules; no processor count.
- I-125 commercial playgrounds (58): municipalities buy by tender (rule 12); one-off, installation and EN 1176.
- I-118 paper carrier bags (49, new): plastic-bag ban gives demand, but Tirana Flexo Print makes them (rule 22).
- I-124 multi-unit letterbox banks (45, new): no evidence that new buildings fit them (rule 5).
