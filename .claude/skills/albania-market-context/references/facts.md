# Albania: detailed facts with sources (checked 22 Nov 2026)

UNVERIFIED = not confirmed from a reliable source. CONFLICT = sources disagree.

## 1. Import rules

- **De minimis €22** for goods bought online and sent by post (goods value only, not shipping or
  insurance, per customs Instruction 2/2019). €45 for gifts between individuals.
  https://dogana.gov.al/english/c/226/online-shopping ·
  https://klsh.org.al/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Raporti-final-Blerjet-online-web-.pdf
  - Above €22 the audit report says parcels pay 20% VAT + "4% customs duty" (flat 4% basis UNVERIFIED).
  - Threshold was cut in Aug 2016 from €150 (audit report, trade.gov) or €145 (Ministry of Economy): CONFLICT.
    https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/albania-ecommerce
  - No 2025–2026 change found. The exemption is for non-commercial parcels to individuals only.
- **VAT** 20% standard; 6% (accommodation, books, TV advertising, a few others); 10% (agricultural inputs);
  medicines exempt.
  https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-shared/docs/services/tax/2026/dttl-tax-albaniahighlights-2026.pdf ·
  https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/albania/corporate/other-taxes
  - **Pending 2027 package** (before Parliament mid-Nov 2026, not confirmed): standard VAT 21%, basic food 6%,
    accommodation 10%, VAT registration threshold ALL 5m (from 10m), dividend tax 8% → 15%.
    https://www.balkanweb.com/?p=3071719 · https://albaniandailynews.com/news/2027-fiscal-package-burdens-businesses-families-dp-mp
- **Customs duty** (WTO applied 2025): simple average 3.5%; 61% of non-agricultural lines duty-free.
  https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/statis_e/daily_update_e/tariff_profiles/al_e.pdf

  | Group | Avg duty | Max |
  |---|---|---|
  | Clothing | 0% | 0% |
  | Electrical machinery / electronics | 2.7% | 10% |
  | Office & computing machinery | 0.2% | 10% |
  | Chemicals (incl. cosmetics) | 1.3% | 6% |
  | Rubber, leather, footwear | 8.4% | 15% |
  | Textiles | 4.4% | 15% |
  | Other manufactures (toys) | 7.3% | 15% |
  | Food preparations | 6.6% | 15% |

  CONFLICT on clothing: an undated Catalan trade portal says 15%
  (https://tradeportal.accio.gencat.cat/ca/mercats-potencials/albania/aranzels). Check each HS code at
  https://dogana.gov.al/preferencat/
- **Nomenclature**: HS-based Combined Nomenclature, updated yearly (Law 8474/1999); Customs Code (Law 102/2014)
  broadly aligned with the EU. https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/albania-import-tariff ·
  EU 2026 report: https://shqiptarja.com/uploads/ckeditor/6aeb230422366albania-report-2026_watermark.pdf
- **Scanning fee**: €22 per declaration over €1,000, €5 below (2021 data); still charged per the EU 2026 report.
- **Excise**: energy products, tobacco, alcohol, roasted coffee, fireworks (PwC link above).
- **Trade agreements** (https://dogana.gov.al/english/d/169/182/249/263/free-trade-agreements):
  EU SAA (Dec 2006), CEFTA 2006 (fully liberalised 2014), Turkey FTA (May 2008, industrial goods), EFTA (2010–11).
  No agreement with China, so MFN rates apply
  (https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/albania-trade-agreements).
  Preferential (duty-free) treatment needs proof of origin: EUR.1 or an origin declaration under the revised
  PEM rules applied from 1 Jan 2025.
  https://www.dogana.gov.al/english/d/182/249/0/413/transitional-rules-of-preferential-origin-of-the-pan-euro-mediterranean-convention

## 2. Product regulation

- Harmonised European standards adopted; certificates from accredited non-EU bodies accepted.
  https://trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/albania-standards-trade
- CE rules from the 2011–12 product-safety and market-surveillance laws (GPSD, Reg. 765/2008, Decision 768/2008);
  main law 10489/2011. https://petosevic.com/node/1458
  Draft amendment to bring online platforms and couriers into scope:
  https://konsultimipublik.gov.al/documents/RENJK_853_Projektligji-shtesa-dhe-ndryshime-ne-Ligj-10489_2011.docx
- Weak enforcement: 895 market-surveillance inspections (400 fewer than before); EU 2026 report calls e-commerce
  "largely unregulated", GPSR not yet aligned. Exact CE duties per product: UNVERIFIED, assume EU rules.
- **Cosmetics**: Law 26/2017 (EU-aligned), safety report per Order 300/2018, GMP per VKM 634/2018.
  https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/albania-labeling-and-marking ·
  https://shendetesia.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/VKM-nr.-634-date-26.10.2018-GMP-1.pdf
  Notification / responsible-person procedure: UNVERIFIED.
- **Food & supplements**: importer licence from AKU; sanitary checks; label notification with lab analysis
  (VKM 434/2018). https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/albania-import-requirements-and-documentation ·
  https://aku.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Licencimi.pdf . Supplement-specific registration: UNVERIFIED, ask AKU.
- **Labels in Albanian** (name, manufacturer/importer address, quantity, expiry where relevant; stickers OK,
  importer applies them). Food labels also need ingredients, storage, importer address.
- **Medicines & medical devices**: registration with AKBPM.
- **E-scooters**: banned from public roads since Oct 2025.
  https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-e-scooter-laws/albania-e-scooter-laws/

## 3. Setting up

- Register online via e-Albania at QKB. https://qkb.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/guide%20RPP.pdf
  shpk 3–7 business days, sole trader 1–3 (https://globallawexperts.com/news/albania-company-formation).
  Minimum shpk capital: ALL 100 (tax office, https://www.tatime.gov.al/eng/shkarko.php?id=1577) vs €1: CONFLICT.
- VAT registration: turnover over ALL 10m, **or if you import** (Deloitte only; confirm with DPT).
- Profit tax: companies 0% up to ALL 14m until 31 Dec 2029, then 15%. Self-employed 0% up to ALL 14m until 2029,
  above that 15% (23% on profit above ALL 14m). Self-employed social 23% + health 3.4% (health on ALL 100k/month in 2026).
  Dividends 8%.
- Fiscalization (Law 87/2019): real-time reporting of every invoice; certified software or fiscal device for B2C
  cash; e-invoices for B2B. https://www.pwc.com/al/en/Law_on_fiscalization.pdf
  Tax office clarification covering online sales delivered by courier and paid on handover:
  https://www.tatime.gov.al/shkarko.php?id=14609 (returned 404; seen in search snippets).
  Instruction 16/2020 on invoice types:
  https://qeverisjavendore.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Udhezim-nr.-16-date-3.4.-2020-Per-faturen-dhe-sistemin-e-monitorimit-te-qarkullimit.pdf
  Courier COD fiscal handling not standardised (audit report): UNVERIFIED in practice.

## 8. Consumer protection

- Law 9902/2008: 14 days to withdraw from distance purchases (from delivery); fulfil orders within 30 days;
  seller liable for defects for 2 years (presumed at delivery within 6 months); answer complaints within
  3 working days. https://erru.al/doc/Consumer_Protection_Law_No.9902,_17.04.2008_English_version.pdf
- Law 50/2025 (CEFTA e-commerce decision): 14-day withdrawal and information duties.
  https://www.pwc.com/al/en/Legal%20Alert%20-%20August%202025.pdf

## Biggest risks for a new importer

1. The €22 exemption helps consumers buying from Temu, not you: commercial imports pay VAT, duty and fees.
2. 2027 changes may raise VAT to 21% and lower the VAT threshold; importers may need VAT registration anyway.
3. Duty-free EU/Turkish goods need proof of origin; Chinese goods via EU/Turkish wholesalers don't qualify.
4. Food, supplements and cosmetics need AKU licences, label notification or safety reports, plus Albanian labels.
5. COD ties up cash and fiscal invoicing on COD is murky; negotiate payout terms and plan for refusals.
6. No Stripe; PayPal uncertain; card payments need a local bank virtual POS.
7. Price competition is fierce (Temu, AliExpress); clothing has 0% duty so there's little local price edge.
8. Shrinking population, but strong lek, tourism and remittances favour B2B (hosts, hotels) and diaspora gifting.
9. Weak enforcement means grey-market rivals, but expect EU-aligned rules to tighten.
