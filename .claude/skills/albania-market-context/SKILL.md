# Albania market context

Facts checked on **22 Nov 2026**. Full details with a source URL for every fact are in
[references/facts.md](references/facts.md). Rules change: before a real decision, re-check anything
marked UNVERIFIED or CONFLICT, and anything older than ~6 months, against the official source.

## The ten facts that change most decisions

1. **Cash on delivery dominates**: >90% of domestic online orders (Balkan eCommerce 2026); other estimates
   50–78%. Plan for COD, refusals and slow cash remittance from couriers.
2. **Consumers' €22 duty/VAT-free parcels** (goods value only, not shipping) let Temu/AliExpress sell
   cheaply. A business importing stock pays full duty + **20% VAT** + scanning fee. Don't compete on cheap
   small items.
3. **Temu is the #1 retail site** (736k monthly visits, ~15k parcels/day in mid-2025), AliExpress #2.
   Local leaders: megateksa.com, shpresa.al, neptun.al, gotech.al, gjirafa50.al, foleja; Merrjep for classifieds.
   People also search a lot for "amazon albania", "amazon de/uk" (Amazon range is hard to get).
4. **Duty is low** (simple average 3.5%; clothing 0%; electronics avg 2.7%; footwear avg 8.4%; toys avg 7.3%),
   and **EU- and Turkish-origin industrial goods are duty-free with proof of origin** (EUR.1 / origin
   declaration, PEM rules). Chinese-made goods pay MFN rates even when bought from an EU/Turkish wholesaler.
   Averages hide peaks: small kitchen appliances (HS 8516.60 air fryers/ovens, 8509.40 blenders, 8509.80
   ice-cream makers, 8516.79) pay **10%** MFN; 8418.69 refrigerating equipment 2% (World Bank WITS, 2022 data:
   `https://wits.worldbank.org/API/V1/SDMX/V21/datasource/TRN/reporter/008/partner/000/product/<HS6>/year/2022/datatype/reported`).
   Always look up the exact HS code. The dogana.gov.al/preferencat search returned nothing for these codes.
   Also budget per shipment: pallet freight, broker (~€80–150) and the €22 scanning fee; on small orders these
   fixed costs can wipe out the margin.
5. **Pending 2027 tax package** (not confirmed law as of 22 Nov 2026): VAT 20% → 21%, VAT registration
   threshold ALL 10m → 5m. Deloitte says importers must register for VAT regardless of turnover (confirm).
6. **Profit tax 0%** for companies and self-employed with income up to ALL 14m until end-2029.
7. **Fiscalization**: every sale needs a real-time fiscal invoice (Law 87/2019), including online/COD sales.
   How couriers handle fiscal invoices on COD is not standardised; ask your accountant.
8. **Payments**: no Stripe; PayPal unclear; local bank virtual POS (e.g. Credins, Raiffeisen) for cards.
   SEPA live since Oct 2025 (cheap EUR transfers to suppliers).
9. **Consumers**: 2.34m people (Tirana 33%), 87% online, 45% bought online in the last year; average gross
   wage ~ALL 90k (~€1,000) a month; €1 ≈ 89.8 ALL (Nov 2026, lek at record strength). Instagram 1.35m,
   Facebook 1.30m, TikTok 1.23m adult reach. 12.5m foreign arrivals (2025), ~27–30k short-term rentals,
   ~€1.1bn remittances, diaspora returns in summer.
10. **Consumer law**: 14-day withdrawal for distance sales, 2-year seller liability for defects, answer
    complaints within 3 working days; Albanian-language labels (stickers allowed) on every unit.

## Product-rule red flags

- **E-scooters**: banned from public roads since Oct 2025. Avoid.
- **Food, supplements**: AKU (National Food Authority) licence and label notification with lab analysis.
- **Cosmetics**: Law 26/2017 (EU-aligned) with a safety report. **Medicines/medical devices**: AKBPM registration.
- **Electrical goods, toys, baby products**: assume EU CE rules apply. Market surveillance is weak, but
  e-commerce rules are expected to tighten as Albania aligns with the EU.
- **Lithium batteries** (power stations, e-bikes): dangerous-goods shipping, so freight costs more.

## Demand drivers worth remembering

- Most power outages in Europe (~40/year), but cheap power (~€0.09/kWh): sell backup on reliability, not savings.
- EVs were 44% of new-car registrations in 2025 (+98%); 1.07m vehicles, Mercedes ~18%.
- Tirana water supply only ~5 h/day in some areas; tap-water quality is disputed.
- Hot, humid coast; long heatwaves; apartment construction boom (many empty, damp flats).
- Tourism boom (short-term-rental hosts are a B2B channel); no Decathlon or IKEA in Albania.
