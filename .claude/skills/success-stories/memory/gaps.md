# Open questions (self-updated each run)

1. How did small sellers in Albania/Kosovo win their first 10–20 customers (online and B2B)?
2. Has anyone sold supplies or "starter kits" to Airbnb/Booking hosts or property managers? What worked,
   what price premium did hosts accept, and how did they find hosts?
3. Stories of small buyers importing Turkish textiles (towels, bed linen): minimum orders, quality problems,
   EUR.1/origin paperwork, freight surprises.
4. Real cash-on-delivery experiences in Albania/Balkans: refusal rates, courier payouts, how sellers cut refusals.
5. First-order sizing: how much stock did successful beginners buy first, and how did over-ordering hurt failures?
6. Pre-selling and deposits: did pre-orders work in markets with low trust in online sellers?
7. Failures: why did small Albanian/Balkan online shops or import businesses close?
8. Competing with cheap local chains (e.g. Megateksa) or Temu: how did small sellers position on quality/service?
9. Running a side business next to a day job: time, what to automate, when to quit the job.
10. Backup niche: experiences becoming a small-market dealer for Chinese smart-home/pet brands (e.g. Petkit).
